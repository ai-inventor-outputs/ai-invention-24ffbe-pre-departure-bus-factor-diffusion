# OSS Community-Health Framing + Bias-Free Corpus Sources

## Part A — OSS-native positioning for "pre-departure authority diffusion"

### A1. CHAOSS Elephant Factor / Contributor Absence Factor

The CHAOSS Contributor Absence Factor (formerly "Bus Factor") is defined precisely: sort contributors by contribution volume descending, sum until reaching 50% of total contributions, and the count of contributors needed is the factor (e.g., factor = 2 if two contributors together exceed 50%) [1]. The CHAOSS knowledge base explicitly notes it "can be measured both ways": as a single-point-in-time **snapshot**, or **longitudinally** by "measur[ing] the factor at regular intervals to observe changes over time" via time-period filters [1]. The Elephant Factor (the organizational-diversity analogue — minimum number of *companies* whose employees account for 50% of commits) is documented as fundamentally a **snapshot metric**: CHAOSS's own text warns "the Elephant Factor will change if one takes a snapshot of any prior time period, so the elephant factor over the life of a product may misrepresent the current level of organizational diversity" [2]. This is the key positioning fact: CHAOSS *names* the longitudinal-tracking option for Contributor Absence Factor but does not ship a standard trend/trajectory construct — practitioners are left to compute and interpret a time series themselves, with no CHAOSS-defined trajectory shape, rate-of-change metric, or pre-departure predictive framing. Neither CHAOSS metric is built around *anticipating* an impending departure; both are retrospective risk indicators computed from current (or a chosen past) contribution distribution. Additional adjacent CHAOSS metrics were found but do not fill this gap: "Newcomer Experience" measures onboarding *welcome quality* [3], "Conversion Rate" measures whether first-time contributors return for a second contribution [3], and "Occasional Contributors" tracks engagement breadth — none of these track authority (commit ownership / DOA-style dominance) diffusing away from a founder over the run-up to their departure.

**Positioning takeaway:** This hypothesis's diffusion-*trajectory* construct is a specific, well-defined instantiation of a measurement CHAOSS explicitly flags as possible ("measure at regular intervals") but does not itself formalize, name, or validate as predictive of anything. It is closer kin to CHAOSS's Contributor Absence Factor than to the Elephant Factor (organizational, not individual), and the honest framing is "operationalizes and validates the longitudinal variant CHAOSS gestures at but does not define," not "invents an unprecedented concept."

### A2. Apache Incubator graduation & the Apache Project Maturity Model

The ASF's own graduation guide states diversity as a hard-ish criterion in prose form: "The project is considered to have a diverse community when it is not highly dependent on any single contributor (there are at least three legally independent committers and there is no single company or entity that is vital to the success of the project)" [4].
